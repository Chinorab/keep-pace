## Interview notes
- Q1 (idea): Training on a mannequin is the core. Emergency mode dropped: rare scenario, a panicked first-time user should face no menu, and it can't be demonstrated honestly. Pitch: training makes bystanders able and willing to act; glasses keep hands busy and eyes on the chest while giving live feedback.
- Flag to resolve later: "progress across sessions" needs memory between sessions, which the preview can't show.
- Concept updated by the user (relayed from the parent session): training-only coach on a mannequin, explicitly NOT an emergency tool. Audience: people who took a CPR course or video but lack practice, and trainers with several students. Goal: the motion becomes automatic.
  New flow: (1) one-line reminder of the three real steps (call, push, defibrillator), then start; (2) user places a target on the mannequin's chest center, Lens checks hands are on it via hand tracking; (3) rhythm cue (visual + sound) anchored at the hands, cadence measured if possible ("Faster", "Slower", "Let the chest rise"), else honest metronome; (4) cue fades across sessions (guided, then only on drift, then silent); (5) end summary with comparison to previous session; (6) optional trainer mode seeing a student's rhythm live.
- Q2 (spatial moment): kneeling at the mannequin's shoulder, hands interlaced on chest center, arms straight, mid second minute, fatigue makes the rhythm drift. Eyes down on the hands. Sees: real mannequin and hands, nothing covering them; a thin ring flat on the chest under the hands, tightens on push, opens on recoil, followed from the corner of the eye; calm ring = on rhythm; when slowing, one word "Faster" above the hands for 1 s, then gone. No red alert, no gauge, no scrolling numbers. Hears a soft click on the beat. No floating panel, no menu, no center counter, max one or two words. "True best moment" is sessions later: ring and click gone, user holds rhythm alone, a small pulse at the end of the set confirms. User's own limit: interlaced fingers may break hand tracking, so ring + click must work with no measurement; "Faster" only if measured cadence is reliable. User's three rules: info lives on the chest; silent when all is well; one word max, never over one second.
- Q2 follow-up: what matters is the guidance fading while the user holds the rhythm alone, not memory. Memory across sessions DROPPED. Fade happens within one set: ring + click full at start; after about ten on-rhythm compressions in a row, ring fades, then click lowers and stops; on drift the ring returns at once, then fades again. One moment, one continuous video take. Needs a reliable cadence signal in the preview: simulated hand tracking or a per-compression stand-in input (gesture or key), stated clearly in the video if a stand-in is used. To settle at Q5 rather than leaving it to CLAD.
- Done criterion from the user (relayed): "In a single session in the Lens Studio preview, the guidance (ring, then click) fades out after about ten consecutive on-pace compressions, comes back immediately when the cadence drifts, and fades again once the user recovers. Memory across sessions is out of scope and listed under 'What's next'." Still needs: the preview-drivable input for compressions (ask at Q5, open question).
- Q3 (product/overlay, stand-ins): overlay on a real manikin, never recognized. User places a chest target with a gesture (works on any manikin or a folded cushion). Preview: simple real-scale placeholder torso lying on the floor, camera lowered to kneeling height; a key press or simple gesture per compression (exact input to settle at Q5). All downstream logic (ring, click, fading) consumes only a target position and a stream of compression events, so real sources (manikin, hand tracking) can replace stand-ins without changing logic. Placeholder torso hidden on device.
- Q4 (placement, starting values): Ring = horseshoe arc flat on chest plane, centered on target, open toward user (nothing over forearms), inner edge >= ~22 cm (surrounds stacked hands ~10x20 cm), breathes 22 cm (push) to 26 cm (release), 1 cm thin low-opacity stroke; click spatialized at target. "Faster" = far side of chest ~10 cm beyond arc, tilted toward eyes, cap height ~2.5 cm (~12 cm wide), 1 s. Opening reminder (Call, Push, Shock) = world-anchored ~1 to 1.2 m ahead while standing, slightly below eye level, above where manikin lies, ~40 cm wide panel, cap height ~4 cm, one icon per line; disappears when target placed, never follows head. Comfort risk: chest ~50 cm from eyes; mitigation: only arc + occasional 1 s word there, all reading done before kneeling; comfort not proven.
- Pushback raised at Q4: at ~55 cm usable view is ~23 cm tall x 38 cm wide; ring 26 cm + 10 cm gap + text ~38 cm deep likely puts "Faster" out of view while looking at the hands. Awaiting user's choice.
- Q4 resolved: arc replaced by two vertical brackets either side of the stacked hands (horizontal axis). Each ~12 cm long, 1 cm stroke, breathe horizontally 7 cm from center (push) to 9 cm (release), always >= 2 cm from hand edge; footprint ~20 x 12 cm. Nothing over hands/forearms. "Faster"/"Slower" become short spoken cues spatialized at the target, at most once every few seconds, only when measured cadence is reliable; brackets briefly thicken at the same time (shape change, not color only). No text near the hands. Click + fading unchanged. Starting values, validate in preview.
- Q5 (hands): (1) place target = single pinch aimed at chest center (mouse pinch on placeholder torso in preview); target = small flat marker for the heel of the hand; brackets center on it; pinching elsewhere moves it; LEAF-testable. (2) compression in preview = each pinch on the placed target (an SIK interactable) emits one compression event; explicit stand-in, no keyboard input; LEAF simulates pinch sequences at a set cadence. (3) hands-on-target check REMOVED (not showable/testable, false alarms worse than silence); flow step 2 = "place the target". What's next: hand-on-target check and on-device compression source (hand motion), adapter-ready but not claimed.
- Q6 (when things show): round = timed 2 min (rescuer switch interval, VERIFY AHA/ERC) or ends early after 3 s without a compression (design choice, not a guideline value). End: brackets one slow expansion then fade, distinct end tone at chest, ~1 s, nothing left on chest; spoken "Round complete" tells user to look up. Summary ~1 s later, world-anchored ~1 m beyond manikin at kneeling eye height, facing user, out of view while looking at hands; 3 lines, cap ~4 cm: round duration, average cadence, regularity (share of compressions within target range, VERIFY range). Measured values only (pinch stand-in in preview, video says so). From round 2 of a launch, one comparison line vs previous round (in-memory, current launch only). Summary stays until user chooses; two rectangular icon+label SIK buttons: "Again" (new round, same target), "Move target". Target locking: before first compression, pinch elsewhere moves target; from first compression to round end target locked, any pinch on torso = compression (hit zone = whole torso), pinch outside torso ignored; after round, move only via "Move target".
- Q7 (done criteria): 5 criteria: fading; target lock; early end (3 s) with summary out of view looking down, readable looking up; faithful summary (known cadence -> matching average, ~100% regularity; irregular lowers it); Again + Move target (comparison line in round 2). What's next: memory across sessions, hands-on-target check, on-device compression detection, shared trainer mode.
